## How it was checked

Every note and structure was written by one reader and confirmed by a second, then reviewed again note by note
against the 9 corrections Layers made to the last hand-off (PR #76): evidence says only what each quote says;
quotes widened to carry their point; `on` is the verse the remark is on; Bullinger's own heading words; "in
Matthew's Greek"; no unnamed dissent in `limit`; the BSB's own poetry lines; our own words; strength by distinct
writers. That review changed 150 of the 164 notes for the 12 forms and Deuteronomy 6:7, and kept all of them.
Then:
- Local quotes word for word in their pinned copies (figcheck --quotes: 0 problems), and every line rule
  (poetrycheck, the build's PR #75 rule: 0 problems).
- Web quotes on a page a checking reader fetched that day.
- The 111 scan quotes (Lowth, Jebb, Boys, Forbes) against archive.org's page text of the leaf named, as fetched:
  exact, or with the scan's long s and a few misread letters allowed but never a missing word.

## Worth knowing

- The Lectures quotes were read in the 1816 printing (archive.org lecturesonsacred02lowt, the same Gregory
  translation), and their links go there; the lowth-lectures work and its shelf card name the 1787 scan.
- Bullinger's printed pages are the search-inside leaf less 54, as in structures-2026-11-01.json. The Riddle
  definition's p. 772 is from the search only.
- Evidence records for scans carry two extra keys: `also_in` (the next leaf, when a quotation runs over the page)
  and `match` (how it matched). The build reads neither.
