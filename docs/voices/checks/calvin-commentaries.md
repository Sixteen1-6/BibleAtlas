## Method

The built Calvin notes (his New Testament notes from the pinned copy, after the reader's cuts) were scanned with the patterns of section 2.5 of docs/local-work/2026-10-20/voices/VOICES-BUILD-SPEC.md: authorship ("not written by", "another hand", "spurious", "interpolat" and the rest), canon ("not canonical", "apocryphal", "straw"), error words ("error", "mistake", "crept in", "amend", "corrupt", "contradict" and the rest) within 60 characters of a word for the text or its writer, and "myth", "legend", "fable". 357 notes had a hit, in 388 paragraphs. Every one of those paragraphs was read in full against the rules below, and every textual point was checked against what the Berean Standard Bible prints.

- Left out: a passage that says a word, name or number of the Bible as the BSB prints it is a mistake, a copyist's error, crept in, corrupt or to be amended; or that a book was not written by the author its opening verse names; or that a biblical account is a myth.
- Kept: textual remarks that agree with what the BSB prints or leaves out; remarks on how the Greek can be read, or that a translation (the Vulgate, Erasmus) is wrong; opinions the text does not settle (who wrote Hebrews); and errors, faults and fables of people, not of Scripture.

Each passage left out is the smallest that makes the claim: one sentence where the paragraph stands without it, else the paragraph. Each is in force at once with the line "A passage is left out here until a decision on it is made." (rule 5), for Shubhanshu to make final.

## Left out (15 passages in 11 notes)

- Matthew 27:9 (sentence): Calvin says the name Jeremiah, which the BSB prints in Matthew 27:9, crept into the text.
- Matthew 27:9 (sentence): Calvin says the name Jeremiah was put down by mistake instead of Zechariah; the rest of the paragraph, on Zechariah 11:13, stands without these two sentences.
- Luke 2:14 (sentence): Calvin calls the genitive reading (men of good will) not genuine and says it crept in, but the BSB prints that same reading (men on whom His favor rests); unsure, since he frames it as a fault of the Vulgate, so please review.
- John 1:28 (sentence): Calvin says the name Bethany in John 1:28, which the BSB prints, is a mistaken insertion for Bethabara (a textual variant; his received text read Bethabara).
- Acts 7:14 (paragraph): Calvin says the number seventy-five, which the BSB prints in Acts 7:14, disagrees with Moses and came in through copyists' error, perhaps altered from what Luke wrote; the whole paragraph argues this.
- Acts 7:16 (sentence): Calvin says the name Abraham in Acts 7:16, as the BSB prints it, is a mistake; the run from 'And whereas he saith afterward' to 'must be amended' (three sentences) belongs together, while the earlier half on the patriarchs' bones offers synecdoche or tradition and can stay.
- Acts 7:16 (sentence): This sentence is Calvin's argument that the name Abraham in Acts 7:16 is wrong; it goes with the sentence before it.
- Acts 7:16 (sentence): Calvin says the text of Acts 7:16 must be amended.
- Acts 7:43 (sentence): Calvin says the word Babylon in Acts 7:43, which the BSB prints, may have crept in by error in place of the prophet's Damascus.
- 2 Corinthians 1:17 (sentence): Calvin suggests the doubled "yes, yes" and "no, no", which the BSB prints in 2 Corinthians 1:17, may have crept into the text through copyists' ignorance; he says it only tentatively, so a reviewer may judge it a mild textual aside.
- 2 Thessalonians 2:4 (sentence): Calvin says the received Greek behind the BSB's "every so-called god" is a corruption of Paul's words; unsure, since the BSB English could fit either reading, but the claim is about the received text.
- 2 Thessalonians 2:4 (sentence): The next sentence makes the same claim, that a transcriber changed the word the BSB translates; leave it out with the one before, and the rest of the paragraph stands.
- James 4:7 (paragraph): Calvin says the quotation "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble" (printed by the BSB in James 4:6) was introduced into many copies and grants it may have crept in from a margin note.
- 1 John 2:23 (paragraph): Calvin omits "whoever confesses the Son has the Father as well", which the BSB prints, as a copyist's note that crept into the text.
- Jude 1:1 (paragraph): Calvin calls the reading "loved by God the Father" corrupt, and that is the reading the BSB prints.

## Kept after a closer look

- Matthew 23:35: Calvin prefers that Jehoiada also bore the name Barachiah and only reports, without adopting, Jerome's view that there is a mistake in the name; borderline because he leaves that possibility open.
- Mark 3:16: 'The word has been corrupted' means Boanerges is an Aramaic name changed in passing into Greek, not that the text is wrong; the BSB prints Boanerges.
- Luke 8:19: Calvin says the Evangelists were not very exact about the order of dates, a remark about arrangement that harmonizes the accounts rather than calling any word wrong.
- Acts 10:30: Calvin faults the Vulgate for omitting 'fasting', a word the BSB also leaves out; he criticises a translation and says no BSB word is wrong, but it disagrees with the BSB's shorter text, so it is borderline.
- John 18:1: Calvin says the Greek article making it 'brook of the cedars' probably crept in; the BSB prints 'Kidron Valley', so his judgement agrees with the BSB.
- Acts 9:7: Calvin mentions others who call the wording a copyist's fault, but rejects that and harmonises Acts 9:7 with 22:9.
- John 21:22: The transcribers' error Calvin names is in the Latin version, not in the Greek text the BSB translates.
- Acts 5:36: Calvin dates Theudas by Fadus and reckons Gamaliel's speech late, a chronological opinion that harmonises rather than faults Luke; his criticism is of Gamaliel's argument.
- John 4:39: Calvin says 'believe' is used loosely (inaccurately) for a beginning of faith; that is a remark on usage, not a claim the word is a mistake.
- John 7:31: Calvin says 'believe' is used loosely for a preparation for faith; a remark on usage, not an error claim.
- John 8:30: Calvin says the Evangelist loosely gives the name of faith to a preparation for faith; a remark on usage, not an error claim.
- John 17:21: Calvin says the Evangelist uses 'believe' loosely for 'know'; a remark on usage, not an error claim.
- Acts 18:4: Calvin says the Latin books' addition that Paul put in the name of Christ crept in; the BSB also lacks it, so his remark agrees with the BSB.
- 1 Corinthians 7:33: Calvin weighs two readings and prefers the one where the married man is divided, which is the reading the BSB prints (7:34, "his interests are divided").
- 1 Corinthians 10:29: Calvin says the repeated "The earth is the Lord's" in some manuscripts crept in from a margin; the BSB omits that repetition in 10:28, so he agrees with it.
- 1 Corinthians 13:1: Calvin blames the transcribers only for where the chapter division falls, which is not part of the inspired text.
- 1 Corinthians 16:15: Calvin calls a second name joined to the house of Stephanas a spurious addition; the BSB names only Stephanas and his household here, so he agrees with it.
- Ephesians 2:5: Calvin wonders whether "by grace you have been saved" was added by another hand but says he gladly receives it as Paul's, so he does not call the BSB's words an error.
- Ephesians 5:9: Calvin prefers "fruit of the light" over "fruit of the Spirit", which is what the BSB prints.
- Philemon 1:7: Calvin thinks "grace" should read "joy", and the BSB prints "joy", so he agrees with it.
- Hebrews 9:1: Calvin says the word "tabernacle" was wrongly added after "first"; the BSB prints "the first covenant" without it, so he agrees with it.
- Hebrews 11:35: Calvin says "they were tempted" crept in by a transcriber; the BSB's Hebrews 11:37 leaves it out, so he agrees with it.
- James 5:16: Calvin notes that some copies have "therefore" and accepts it; the BSB prints "Therefore".
- Hebrews 2:3: Calvin argues Paul did not write Hebrews, an opinion the text does not settle since the letter names no author.
- Jude 1:9: Calvin reports that others gave Jude less weight for using an apocryphal story and himself defends it as handed-down history, so he does not deny the letter.
- Jude 1:14: Calvin holds Enoch's prophecy was handed down unwritten rather than taken from an apocryphal book, which upholds Jude.
- Colossians 4:16: Calvin says the letter from Laodicea was not Paul's and that a later "Epistle to the Laodiceans" is a forgery; this concerns no book of the Bible.

All other hits are about the errors, corruptions or fables of people (the Papists, the Pharisees, heretics, the old Latin translator), or places where Calvin shows that the Gospel accounts agree.
