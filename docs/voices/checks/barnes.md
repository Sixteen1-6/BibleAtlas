# Scripture check: Albert Barnes

What was checked, how, and what was decided, for `config/voices/barnes-nt.json` (decision 23, rules 3 to 5 and 7). The notes are from Albert Barnes's *Notes, Explanatory and Practical, on the New Testament* (1832 to 1852), as CrossWire's Barnes module 1.1 keeps them; the module names no source and no printing, and its John 3:16 note differs from the 1858 printing of the Gospels in ways that suggest a later one (docs/local-work/2026-10-20/voices/voices-research-results.json). So the book line reads "in a printing after 1858 that this copy does not name" (decision 23, rule 7).

## What is not shown at all

- His preface to the Gospels, his general introduction to the New Testament and his introductions to the books (34 of them, 509,471 characters): the reader starts at the comment on each book's first verse, or at his analysis of its first chapter, found by the words in `starts`. The introductions are where his arguments for each book's writer and date sit; he defends the traditional writer throughout, and they were not checked passage by passage.
- The lists of references and marginal readings keyed to the King James text, which the module adds after a note ("(n) "For God" 1Jn 4:9"); the genealogy charts drawn in text at Matthew 1; the notes the module's maker added; the verse labels.

## Method

The notes as the reader keeps them were scanned for authorship words ("not written by", "not the work of", "later hand", "pseudonym", "forged", "spurious", "interpolat" and the rest), canon words ("apocryphal", "not canonical", "straw"), "myth", "legend", "fable", textual words ("spurious", "not genuine", "genuineness", "authenticity", "wanting in", "omitted in", "not found in", "not in the original", "MSS", "manuscripts", "Griesbach", "doubtful" and the rest), and error words ("error", "mistake", "crept in", "corrupt", "copyist", "transcrib" and the rest) within 60 characters of a word for the text or its writer. The scan flagged 647 paragraphs in 543 notes. Every one was read in full against the rules below, next to what the King James Version and the Berean Standard Bible print at that verse, with the BSB's footnotes.

- Left out: a passage that says a word, name, number or verse of the Bible, as the BSB prints it, is a mistake, a copyist's error, spurious, corrupt, interpolated or not genuine; that a book was not written by the writer its opening names; that what the text reports as history did not happen; or that a biblical account is a myth.
- Kept: textual remarks that agree with what the BSB prints or leaves out (Barnes often doubts the very words the BSB leaves out: Acts 8:37, the "three in heaven" of 1 John 5:7, the doxology of Matthew 6:13, Luke 17:36, the King James subscriptions to the letters); the objections of critics and unbelievers that he reports in order to answer them; remarks on the Greek, or that the King James wording is less exact; opinions the text does not settle (who wrote Hebrews, the Gospels' writers); difficulties the note harmonises; and the errors, fables and forgeries of people.

Each passage left out is the smallest that makes the claim, normally one sentence, and is shown as "[passage left out]" with the line "A passage is left out here until a decision on it is made." (decision 23, rule 5). Shubhanshu makes each one final. A passage marked unsure is one where Barnes prefers another reading without deciding, or where the claim against the BSB's words is slight.

## Kept after a closer look

- 1 Corinthians 15:51: Barnes says the apostles may have been mistaken about when the world would end. He is answering the objection that Paul expected to live until Christ came, and he says Scripture gives no date; the mistaken saying of John 21:23 is the brethren's, which the verse itself corrects.
- John 5:4, Acts 23:15, 1 Corinthians 10:28, Hebrews 11:37, Revelation 6:1: Barnes defends King James words the BSB leaves out or puts in a footnote, but calls no word the BSB prints a mistake.
- Acts 7:1: he says Stephen may not have been inspired in every word and that Luke reports the speech faithfully; this defends the text.
- Ephesians 4:8: he holds that Psalm 68 first spoke of the ark and that Paul applies it to Christ; a view on interpretation.
- Revelation 1:8: he prefers the reading "God" to "Lord"; the BSB prints "Lord God", which holds both.
- Jude 1:9: "looks like a mere Jewish fable" states the difficulty, which the next paragraphs answer in Jude's defence.
- 2 Peter 2:11, 2:12 and 2:15, 1 Timothy 1:17: he reports that words are missing in some manuscripts, or that a name was later corrupted outside the Bible, without rejecting what the BSB prints.

The other flagged paragraphs were plainly within the kept list above.

## Letters put right

The module keeps 68 bytes in the old DOS code page 437. The build reads them by that code page, so the pound sign, the degree sign and one-half show as Barnes printed them. The 47 that stand for another letter (the letter f in "of", the letter t, a degree sign printed as a cent sign, the o of Kuinoel's name, and five stray marks) are put right as `repairs` in config/voices/barnes-nt.json lists them, and the build fails if any is no longer found.
