# Scripture check: Matthew Henry, the complete commentary

What was checked, how, and what was decided, for `config/voices/henry-complete.json` (decision 23, rules 3 to 5). The notes are Henry's own from Genesis to Acts, and from Romans to Revelation those of the ministers who finished his Exposition after his death in 1714.

## Method

The notes as the reader keeps them (each section's comment, after the King James verses it prints, the prefaces and the chapter outlines are cut) were scanned with the patterns of section 2.5 of docs/local-work/2026-10-20/voices/VOICES-BUILD-SPEC.md: authorship ("not written by", "another hand", "spurious", "interpolat" and the rest), canon ("not canonical", "apocryphal", "straw"), error words ("error", "mistake", "crept in", "amend", "corrupt", "contradict" and the rest) within 60 characters of a word for the text or its writer, and "myth", "legend", "fable". 373 notes had a hit, in 423 paragraphs. Every one of those paragraphs was read in full against the rules below, and each textual point was checked against what the Berean Standard Bible prints.

- Left out: a passage that says a word, name or number of the Bible as the BSB prints it is a mistake, a copyist's error, crept in, corrupt or to be amended; or that a book was not written by the author its title or opening verse names; or that a biblical account is a myth.
- Kept: textual remarks that agree with what the BSB prints or leaves out; remarks on how the Hebrew or Greek can be read, or that a translation (the Septuagint, the Vulgate, the King James wording) is wrong; opinions the text does not settle; an apparent difficulty that the note harmonises; and errors, faults, corruptions, fables and forgeries of people, not of Scripture.

Most hits are about people: sinners' errors, corrupt hearts, false teachers, the Jews' and the heathen's fables, forged lies, the Maccabees and the Apocrypha named as history outside the Bible, and "straw" as straw.

Each passage left out is the smallest that makes the claim: one sentence where the paragraph stands without it. Each is in force at once with the line "A passage is left out here until a decision on it is made." (rule 5), for Shubhanshu to make final.

## Left out (6 sentences in 3 notes)

- 1 Chronicles 9:35 (four sentences, shown as one mark): Henry says that, if copyists' errors are allowed in the originals, he would think 9:35-44, which repeats 8:29-38 and which the BSB prints, came in by a copyist's slip; the paragraph's first sentence, that the verses repeat 8:29-38, stays.
- 2 Chronicles 16:1 (sentence): following Josephus, Henry says the thirty-sixth year (16:1) and the thirty-fifth (15:19), which the BSB prints, are a transcriber's mistake for the twenty-sixth and twenty-fifth.
- Nehemiah 7:5 (sentence): Henry puts the differences between the numbers in Nehemiah 7 and Ezra 2, both printed by the BSB, down first to copyists' mistakes; the next sentence, which harmonises the two lists another way, stays. Unsure, since copyists' error is one of the reasons he offers.

## For Shubhanshu: 1 John 5:7 (kept for now)

The note on 1 John 5:6-12 argues, over seven paragraphs, that the words "the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one" (1 John 5:7 in the King James Version) are genuine and were lost from some copies, and then explains them. The BSB does not print those words (it notes them as found only in late manuscripts). Henry does not say any word the BSB prints is wrong; he defends more words than it prints. That is not what decision 23 leaves out, so the note is kept whole; the opposite case (a writer leaving out words the BSB prints, Calvin on 1 John 2:23) was left out. If these paragraphs should be left out too, they are paragraphs 6 to 12 of that note.

## Kept after a closer look

- 2 Chronicles 22:2: Henry says "forty-two" is a copyist's slip for twenty-two; the BSB prints twenty-two, so he agrees with it.
- 2 Chronicles 36:9: Henry says "eight" is a slip for eighteen; the BSB prints eighteen.
- 1 Chronicles 22:14: Henry only reports that most interpreters allow an error in the talents, and himself keeps the figures as a large round sum.
- Judges 5:26: a remark on the King James English, "smote off his head"; the BSB reads "crushed his head", as Henry prefers.
- Esther 9:20: "many think it was not written by Mordecai"; Esther names no writer, so this is an opinion the text does not settle.
- Psalm 96:10: a gloss ("The Lord reigneth from the tree") that crept into some early writers' text; the BSB does not print it, so he agrees with it.
- Isaiah 7:1: some call the "sixty-five years" of 7:8 a transcriber's mistake; Henry rejects that and explains the number, which the BSB prints.
- Jeremiah 27:1: some call "Jehoiakim" an error of the copy for "Zedekiah"; the BSB prints Zedekiah, and Henry also gives a way to reconcile the two.
- Daniel 12:5: Henry grants the histories do not show the 1,290 days to the day, then says the prophecy was no doubt fulfilled exactly.
- Matthew 4:1: "not that one scripture contradicts another", with the two verses shown to agree.
- John 4:4: place names "are commonly corrupted by tract of time": Shechem became Sychar in people's speech; the BSB prints Sychar.
- Acts 7:42: "Jesus" in Acts 7:45 is to be read as Joshua, a note on the King James wording; the BSB prints Joshua.
- Romans 3:1: later copies of the Septuagint took Romans 3:13-18 into Psalm 14; the BSB's Psalm 14 does not have those lines, so the remark agrees with it.
- 1 Corinthians 7 and James 2: an apparent contradiction raised and then resolved; 1 John 3:9 is reconciled with 1 John 1:9 the same way.
- 2 Timothy 3:8: Jannes and Jambres are found in old Jewish writers, which doubts nothing in the text.
