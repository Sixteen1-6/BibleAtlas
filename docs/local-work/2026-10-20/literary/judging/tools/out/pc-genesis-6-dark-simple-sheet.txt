God's sorrow, and God who does not change

Genesis 6:6 with Numbers 23 and 1 Samuel 15 · Literary devices

✕

One Hebrew word, nacham, runs through these verses. Scripture says the LORD “regretted”, and that He “is not a man, that He should change His mind”. It holds both together.

Genesis
Numbers
1 Samuel
Genesis 6:6And the LORD regretted that He had made man on the earth, and He was grieved in His heart.
Genesis 6:7for I am grieved that I have made them.
Numbers 23:19God is not a man, that He should lie, or a son of man, that He should change His mind.
1 Samuel 15:11I regret that I have made Saul king,
1 Samuel 15:29Moreover, the Glory of Israel does not lie or change His mind, for He is not a man, that He should change His mind.
1 Samuel 15:35and the LORD regretted that He had made Saul king over Israel.

Believing writers read the feeling in two ways. Study shows both, with their writers.

What it is

In Bullinger's words: This figure is used of the ascription of human passions, actions, or attributes to God Figures of Speech Used in the Bible (1898), p. 871

Who names it

widely agreed · Named by Bullinger, Matthew Henry, Keil and Delitzsch, John Gill, John Calvin, John Wesley and Joseph Benson.
