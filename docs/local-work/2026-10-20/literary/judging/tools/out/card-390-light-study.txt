Literary devices, one card per device

A Literary devices card sits beside the Layers card at Study and Deep: one entry per device on the selected verse, each with the BSB words, one plain line, its sources and a see the whole structure view drawn in the map's own arcs. At Simple there is no card, only a quiet line under the verse when the device is self-evident. At every level the original sources are named: the writers who state it on the verse, each a link to read him, and the book that defines the form, quoted with its page. Nothing credits the app or AI.

What this state shows: Psalm 1:6, Study, phone
The card sits in the Links panel where a Layers card would be (Psalm 1:6 has none). Two entries: the verse's own opposite lines, and the whole psalm's two ways.
Each entry: the BSB words with the answering words in the app's colour pairs, one plain line, the strength, the Hebrew the BSB hides (one word, derekh, for both “path” and “way”) and its sources: each writer who names it, linked to his page on this verse, and the form in Bullinger's words, linked to its page in the 1898 scan.
Phone: the card is in the Explore pane; the line under the verse is in Read. “See the whole structure” rises as a bottom sheet over both.
How the card decides what shows
Simple
No card. A line under the verse only when the device is self-evident in the BSB (a “but” between two lines, bookends, a refrain) or widely agreed. Plain words, no terms, and the writers' names under it.
Study
The card: BSB words, one line, strength, the Hebrew the English hides, both readings where believing writers differ, and its sources.
Deep
Each writer's own words with a link, the old name for the form, how it was checked, structures only one writer sees, and a way to suggest a correction.
Sources
At every level: who names it on this verse, each name a link to read him there, and what the form is in the words of the book that defines it (Bullinger, 1898), with a link to that page of the scan. Where no old book of figures names the device, the card says so. The app never names itself or AI as a source.
Strength
The count of cite-list writers who state the point: 3 or more widely agreed, 2 commonly held, 1 some interpreters. Bullinger counts once. The defining book is not counted unless it names this verse.

Each entry rests on the writers named under it, quoted from their own books, and each form is defined in the words of Bullinger's Figures of Speech Used in the Bible (1898), with its page. Words: the Berean Standard Bible; Hebrew: STEPBible TAHOT. Every BSB quotation, of any length, is checked word for word when the data is built.

What the mockup holds to
Every Scripture word is the BSB, copied from the pinned BSB.json and checked by script: 70 strings, each found word for word in its verse.
Every writer's quotation was matched by script against his own text: 36 quotations from Bullinger, Matthew Henry, Keil and Delitzsch, Gill, Calvin, Clarke, Spurgeon, Jamieson, Fausset and Brown, Wesley, Henry's Concise Commentary and Benson. All are on the cite list.
No rabbi or Jewish scholar is cited or shown. Where Henry and Gill pass on a Jewish tradition (on 1 Samuel 2:5), it is left out.
Bullinger gives figure names and layouts only, never his doctrine; his note on Psalm 19 that points to his book on the stars is not shown.
Every definition is Bullinger's own sentence, found word for word in the Figures of Speech e-text and on its printed page of the 1898 Princeton scan (archive.org figuresofspeechu00bull). The page numbers come from the scan's own page map and BookReader data (page 351 is leaf 405), so each “p.” link opens that page.
The Hebrew words, link counts, strongest links, the Layers cards and the map's arcs are the live site's own data (build 7bd9f13db417), not invented.
Hebrew facts the notes rely on were checked in the data: one word for “path” and “way” in Psalm 1:6; the crossed names in 1 Samuel 1:2; nacham in all six verses of the Genesis 6:6 view; bahem in Psalm 19:4 and 19:11.
Violet is new: the card's edge, its kind labels, the line under the verse and the device arcs on the map share it, as pink and blue already mark quotations and parallels.
Two research counts were raised after reading the writers again: Psalm 1:6 has three writers on the verse itself (Henry, Keil and Delitzsch, Gill), and Hannah and Peninnah gain Matthew Henry, who states the contrast plainly.
No line anywhere credits AI, following the founder's answer of 2026-10-20 (“just credit original source”). The Layers card's live line about who drafted its notes is shown here as “Drawn from …”, naming only the sources, and its notes name their writers at every level.
Questions this raises for the founder
Is violet right for literary devices, or should the card share the Layers gold?
May Bullinger's figure names (Epanodos, Prosopopoeia, Anthropopatheia) appear at Deep, with plain names everywhere else?
Should a structure only one writer sees (Psalm 19 in four parts) appear at all, at Deep, as “a structure some readers see”?
On God's grief in Genesis 6:6, is showing both believing readings, each with its writers, the right answer?
Should Simple show a device line at all, or should the whole feature begin at Study?
Bullinger is the one book of figures from 1930 or earlier that defines every form shown here. May Keach and De Laune's Tropologia (1681; CC0, already on GitHub) stand beside him as a second definer for God in human terms and things spoken of as persons?
