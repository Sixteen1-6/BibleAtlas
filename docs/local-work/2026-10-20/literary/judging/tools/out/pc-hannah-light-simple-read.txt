3Year after year Elkanah would go up from his city to worship and sacrifice to the LORD of Hosts at Shiloh, where Eli’s two sons, Hophni and Phinehas, were priests to the LORD.23 links ›
4And whenever the day came for Elkanah to present his sacrifice, he would give portions to his wife Peninnah and to all her sons and daughters.9 links ›
5But to Hannah he would give a double portion, for he loved her even though the LORD had closed her womb.11 links ›
6Because the LORD had closed Hannah’s womb, her rival would provoke her and taunt her viciously.14 links ›
7And this went on year after year. Whenever Hannah went up to the house of the LORD, her rival taunted her until she wept and would not eat.5 links ›
8“Hannah, why are you crying?” her husband Elkanah asked. “Why won’t you eat? Why is your heart so grieved? Am I not better to you than ten sons?”14 links ›
9So after they had finished eating and drinking in Shiloh, Hannah stood up. Now Eli the priest was sitting on a chair by the doorpost of the temple of the LORD.9 links ›
10In her bitter distress, Hannah prayed to the LORD and wept with many tears.25 links ›
