## Summary

Most of what Bible Atlas shows beyond the Bible's own words is either unsourced where most readers see it, or credited to the website or dataset it came through instead of the person who wrote it, and almost nothing carries a date. The worst gap is AI-drafted text shown at Simple and Study with no source and no AI line: 3,095 Layers notes (including the first verse every visitor sees, Isaiah 53:5), 694 Wordplay notes whose 1,766 citations are never shown at any level, 1,584 Ask the Bible chains, the Aramaic notes (credited to the BSB and STEPBible), 148 theme descriptions and the parallels notes. The first screen tells readers that "Bible readers" linked the arcs, when most of the 344,799 links come from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, which no credit in the app names. Where credits exist they mostly name a host: Bible Hub's "Barnes" on Genesis is James G. Murphy and on the Minor Prophets E. B. Pusey; "Matthew Henry" heads books finished by other ministers; the Tyndale study notes and the UBS articles name no writer; and about 4,250 Hebrew definitions are credited to Brown-Driver-Briggs although they are STEPBible's own notes. No source anywhere shows both its original date and the date it was read (0 of 58 shelf cards, 0 of 6,014 Layers citations), and many credits cannot be followed or understood: 535 Layers links open a whole book, "All sources" opens the top of the shelf, and codes such as TAHOT, (AS), N:N-M-P and "Hom. iv. [iii.] in Joan" are shown raw. The true-credits branch, still in research, will give Layers citations their true writer per book, original dates and honest read dates, and the writers-at-Deep spec gives each Christian writer a checked per-volume credit line and names the copy used; neither touches the Simple and Study levels, the AI-drafting lines, the cross-reference credit, the word study or the dataset dates, so the largest gaps are still open. One shared citation shape and one credit component, built first, would close most of them.

## How to read this

- **Order.** Group A is claims shown with no source at all (or a credit that names the wrong maker), most misleading first. Group B is credits that name a website, dataset or one figurehead instead of the person who wrote the words. Group C is credits a reader cannot follow or understand: no dates, no exact link, codes, hover-only text. Group D is credits that conflict with the founder's cite and avoid lists. Within each group, gaps that reach more readers at a lower level come first.
- **Levels.** Simple, Study, Deep, as the app names them. "At Simple" means every reader sees it.
- **Size.** S: a few lines in one to three files, under half a day with checks. M: a data field, build code, UI and verify for one surface, one to three days. L: needs reading or research per item (a workflow over several days).
- **Covered by.** TC: the true-credits change (branch `true-credits`, Layers citations only; research in progress, no commits of its own yet past `0da9d1c`, so coverage is taken from its notes in `credits\dates.md`). LM: the Layers mend, PR #50, open, the base of TC. WD: the planned writers-at-Deep work (`voices\VOICES-BUILD-SPEC.md`, not started). "None" means no planned work covers it.

## Build this first: one citation shape and one credit line

Most fixes below say "use the shared shape". It is worth building once, before the per-surface fixes.

**The shape** (one per citation, in config and in the shipped data):

| Field | What it holds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `writer` | The person who wrote the words, or `"unsigned"` with the publisher | `John Gill`; `unsigned, Got Questions Ministries` |
| `about` | Who the writer was, a few words | `Baptist pastor, London` |
| `work` | Title and kind of work | `An Exposition of the Old Testament (verse-by-verse commentary)` |
| `part` | Where in the work | `on Genesis 3:15`; `vol. 1, p. 120`; `section 2.31` |
| `written` | Original date of the words | `about 1763 to 1766` |
| `edition` | The printing or translation the words are in, when it matters | `Larry Pierce's modernised text` |
| `read_from` | Where the app read it | `Bible Hub`; `STEPBible TBESG, commit 1f3423d4 of 5 Oct 2026` |
| `read_on` | The date the app truly read it, or `{checked_by, pr}` when only a later check is proven, or `"not recorded"` | `2026-10-20` |
| `url` | The exact page or entry, never a home page | `https://biblehub.com/commentaries/gill/genesis/3.htm` |
| `quote` | The exact words (public domain) or a pointer (copyrighted), kept in the repository so anyone can re-check | |

**The line, by level.** Simple: "From John Gill (1760s)." Study: "John Gill, Exposition of the Old Testament (about 1763 to 1766), on Genesis 3:15." Deep: "John Gill, Baptist pastor in London, An Exposition of the Old Testament (about 1763 to 1766), on Genesis 3:15. Read on Bible Hub, in Larry Pierce's modernised text, 20 October 2026." with the link. For text the app wrote, the same component adds: "Drafted with AI help (Claude), October 2026. Not yet reviewed by a person."

**Build checks.** Every citation has a writer or "unsigned"; every citation has `read_on` or says it is not recorded; no `url` is a work's home page; no strength word is above the count of distinct cited writers; every writer named in a note's evidence is cited; the avoid list runs over every config file that names a writer (not only `config/layers.json`); every credit component names a work id; every block of app-written text has an AI line with a month.

**Files.** `config/layer-sources.json`, `crates/atlas-cli/src/layers.rs` (CiteSpec, lines 107 to 121, and the output, 339 to 356), `web/src/data/layers.ts` (Cite, 30 to 38), a new credit component beside `SourceNote` in `web/src/ui/extras/kit.tsx`, `sources.json` (dates, see C1). **Size** M. **Covered by** TC for the Layers data (writer, original date, read label); the display below Deep, the AI line and the checks are not in its notes.
